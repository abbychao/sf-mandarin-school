SF Mandarin School

SFUSD is opening its first-ever Mandarin immersion TK–8 school in fall 2027. We are the parents and community members supporting the launch.

Where would you enroll long-term?

The school is launching at 1670 Noe St, but only for one year. The permanent site will be announced in January 2027, and we are part of the district’s working group recommending a permanent site.

Your response helps us recommend a location that is accessible for as many families as possible.

Our Work

Building With SFUSD

We hold monthly meetings with district leadership. Together, we’ve identified the areas where our group can have the most impact:

Permanent Site Advocacy

We’re part of the district’s monthly working group (October–January), advocating for a permanent site families across San Francisco can reach.

Parent Engagement

Keeping families connected and informed about progress, timelines, and ways to get involved as the school takes shape.

Teacher Recruiting

Finding exceptional Mandarin immersion educators who share our vision for world-class bilingual education. View the job description →

Fundraising

Rallying community resources to ensure the school opens with the programs, materials, and support our children deserve.
